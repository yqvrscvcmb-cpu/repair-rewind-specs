# Learn AR with SPECS — build decision

Research date: 22 October 2026. Primary sources are the [overview](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/), [rules](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/rules), [resources](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/resources), and [official planning pack](https://github.com/challengepost/learn-specs).

## What judges reward

The four equally weighted criteria are Design, Technical Implementation, Innovation/Idea and Presentation. Tie-breaking follows that order. The strategic implication is to make one complete, comfortable interaction work, and show it clearly. Extra features do not compensate for a broken flow.

## Required delivery

- New SPECS Lens started in the submission window, planned with the Learn skill pack, built through CLAD and Lens Studio MCP.
- Runs in Lens Studio Preview; glasses are not required.
- Public repository containing the Lens Studio project and `devpost/` planning material.
- Public YouTube demo under three minutes, plus a short English description of purpose, audience and learning.
- Deadline: **9 November 2026, 17:00 EST / 23:00 Europe/Amsterdam**.
- Reviewers judge the video rather than cloning and running the repository.

Final eligibility, account registration, public publication and submission remain separate actions. This document is a source-based checklist, not an assertion that the entry is eligible or submitted.

## Why this concept

The user selected a repair companion and delegated engineering/design details. Repair Rewind focuses on a specific problem: remembering the order and location of parts after an interruption. A phone photo preserves appearance; a spatial breadcrumb preserves the relationship between part, place and sequence while hands remain available.

Existing [FIXIT](https://devpost.com/software/fixit-owld5e) and [BrickSight](https://devpost.com/software/bricksight) show that generic AR repair/building guidance is already explored. Our proposed difference is reversible memory of the user's own explicit actions, with uncertainty and manual confirmation. This is a differentiation hypothesis, not a claim of worldwide novelty or patentability.

## Scope chosen for a strong first demo

A disconnected mechanical teaching assembly, three parts, one tray, one spatial origin per part. Select a part using standard SIK input, then choose Remember & lift to record its original pose before the simulated lift. Reassembly advances in reverse recorded order. A pause collapses overlays; resume restores the same pending step. All three parts are visibly distinguishable by shape and number, not color alone.

No remote AI inference is needed at runtime. Do not fabricate missing steps or use generated repair advice. Keep the prototype local and geometric.
